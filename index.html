The Facets

OUTDOORS

The size, the scale, the colors, the feeling. Growing up in the outdoors I've fallen in love with rivers and mountains. The people that they attract and the experiences they provide never fail to inspire. Telling the stories, archiving the joy and cataloging the thoughts - thats what happens here.

ARTS

When we collect various art forms in one place they tend to influence and play with one another, creating and inspiring lives of their own. Alongside one antoher, here is the playgroud of the creative acts of my life, this site acting as the bridge.

AEROSPACE

Originally inspired by flight and and its magic-like qualities, the awe has yet to dwiddle after a bachelors and soon to be master in aerospace engineering. The beauty of the math, elegence of the dynammics, and qualities of the people within this niche world are intruiging and addicting. Here, I share stories, draw connections, and reflect on the experiences that have shaped me so far.
