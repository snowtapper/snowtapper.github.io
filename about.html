ABOUT THIS PLACE

Here, outside social media, away from comptetiion, worry of preception, or misrepresenting the moment, is where we come to explore creative acts. I believe that creative acts come from all corners of life and each can reflect beauty and intention.

Aspects of content represented here come in a wide variety of forms and figures. Whether that be writtn, visual, physical, philosophical, or even just a passing moment caught between stangers that felt striking. Sources of inspiration for me can come from my studies and work in aerospace, which is seemingly quantitative and technial, but can often leave me mesmorized and inspired. Addiionally, the outdoors serve as never-ending source of newness and life. Constantly trying to get that tantilizing feeling of being the small in the great big and beautiful and the feelings these wild places can evoke.

This site is and was built as an archive and display of the content and experiences I found in my daily life, create myself, or explore with others. All the writting, visuals, and reflects are my own, and although this means they are imperfect at times, this makes them real. In an effort to avoid machine made art and perfection, this space is a breeding ground for pure, innocent creating for the the joy of creating.

My story

Loving the outdoors has been engrained in me since day one. I grew up in the mountains of Bend, Oregon and had the privilege of skiing before class, climbing at sunrise, week long river trips in the summers, hikes outside my door, and a community that put value on the wild. The Deschutes River ran directly through town and given our frequent family trips on this river, the Rogue River, Salmon River (Main and Middle fork) and the McKenzie River, I see incedible symbolism in the water.
